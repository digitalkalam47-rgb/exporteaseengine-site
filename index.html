What it does

For manufacturers and merchant exporters, in any product. The work of an export deal, from first market to repeat order, in one place.

Find HS code

Describe your product and get likely codes from the official Indian list, with export rules and benefits. Confirm with your customs broker.

Find countries

See where your product sells, who buys it there, and why a country may suit you.

Run ads

Make ads for the countries you choose. Nothing is spent until you say yes.

Chats

Talk to buyers on WhatsApp from one screen, with your team.

Follow-ups

Export deals take months. Polite reminders go out at the buyer's local time, and stop when the buyer replies or says stop.

My buyers

Every buyer and every stage of the deal, so nobody gets forgotten.

Send price

Make a quote or proforma invoice with your name on it, and turn a quote into a PI in one step.

My website

A simple website for your buyers, with your products on it.

How it works

  1. Add your products

    With HS code, photos, prices and minimum order.

  2. Choose where to sell

    Compare countries and pick the ones that suit you.

  3. Reach buyers

    Bring enquiries into WhatsApp, from ads or your own contacts.

  4. Close the order

    Send a price, keep following up, and move the deal to payment.
